The Cosmic Archive Experience

Engineered from the ground up to replace flat static catalog websites with an evocative, spatial 3D learning metaverse.

Procedural 3D Environment

Traverse 5 distinct architectural chambers engineered with custom Three.js geometries, PBR lighting, and soft shadows—no flat simulation or placeholder images.

360° Inspection Studio

Rotate, zoom, and analyze 32+ historical artifacts with wireframe toggles, multi-spectrum lighting presets, and synthesized audio guide narrations.

Spatial Portal Teleportation

Venture smoothly between historical antiquity, space exploration, and cybernetic art dimensions through glowing physical wormhole portals.

Automated Guided Tours

Relax in cinematic tour mode as camera trajectories glide gracefully between flagship exhibits accompanied by voice narration and progress tracking.

Interactive Quiz Arena

Test your historical mastery across 22 multi-disciplinary educational questions with instant feedback, streak bonuses, and permanent achievement awards.

Offline Persistent State

Demonstration accounts, user exploration logs, custom administrator-curated relics, and personal bookmarks are saved locally using HTML5 localStorage.
